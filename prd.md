## At least 50 animals find forever homes through the app within the first 3 months.
## • Home Screen: Full-screen swipeable pet cards with photo, name, age, and distance.
• Pet Profile: Detailed bio, vaccination status, personality tags, and shelter info.
• Matches & Chat: List of liked pets with a direct button to message the shelter.
## Must-have • Animal profile cards (photos, age, size, short bio).
• Swipe interface (Like / Pass).
• Basic search filters (dog/cat, age, size).
• Direct contact or chat button to connect with the shelter. nice-to-have • Lifestyle compatibility quiz for adopters.
• Short video stories of the animals playing.
• Interactive map of nearby shelters.
• One-tap donation button to support shelter food and medical care.
## Petfinder / Adopt-a-Pet: For detailed animal profiles, health tags, and reliable shelter connections.
## Who it's for Adopters: Families, couples, and individuals looking for a loyal lifelong friend.
# Connects stray and shelter animals with loving future owners through a simple, joyful matching system.
## Mobile app first (iOS & Android). A mobile-first approach allows adopters to browse pets anytime, anywhere, and lets shelter volunteers easily take and upload photos directly from their phones and computers.
## • Warm Coral Orange (#FF6B4A): Represents warmth, energy, and friendliness (primary buttons and accents).
• Soft Cream (#FFFDF9): Clean, gentle background that makes pet photos stand out.
• Deep Teal (#2A6F68): Represents trust, care, and safety (headers and icons).
## • It will not allow commercial breeders to sell pets for profit.
• It will not process online payments for buying animals.
• It will not serve as a general social network or forum for unrelated pet memes.
